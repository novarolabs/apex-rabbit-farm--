About Apex Rabbit Farm

Raising Quality. Growing Opportunity.

Who We Are

Apex Rabbit Farm is a growing rabbit-farming enterprise based in Nyeri, Kenya, near Kamuyu Dispensary. We raise rabbits for sale, keep selected breeding stock, and collect manure and urine as natural farm by-products.

We also offer a breeding service for farmers who want to bring their own rabbit to breed with our stock, and we share practical rabbit-farming information for free, for anyone starting out or looking to improve their own setup.

We're a small, honest farm — we don't overstate our inventory, invent breed names, or make guarantees we can't keep. What we list is what we actually have or can genuinely offer.

What We Offer

Rabbits for Sale

Young and mature rabbits, depending on availability.

Breeding Rabbits

Selected stock for farmers building or expanding a rabbitry.

Farm By-Products

Rabbit manure and urine for agricultural use.

Farming Guidance

Practical, free guidance for rabbit farmers.
